# AI adoption barriers

The reasons firms give for not using AI. They show what a firm would need to know or have before it starts.

## What the sources say

- Three quarters of Dutch businesses do not use AI and have not considered it [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Of the businesses that considered AI but do not use it, 73% name lack of experience as the main reason [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Privacy (49%) and unclear legal consequences, such as liability for damage (42%), also play a role [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- 15% say they do not find AI useful [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
