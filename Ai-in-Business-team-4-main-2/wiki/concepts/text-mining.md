# Text mining

AI that analyses written text, for example to read documents and pull out the relevant facts. It is the technology behind reading invoices and e-mails automatically.

## What the sources say

- Text mining is the most used AI technology among Dutch businesses: 12% of businesses in 2025 [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Its use grew from 4% in 2023 to 8% in 2024 and 12% in 2025 [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- The next most used technologies are natural language generation (8%) and speech recognition (6%, tripled since 2023) [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
