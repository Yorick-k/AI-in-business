# AI adoption by firm size

How the share of firms that use AI changes with the number of people they employ. Smaller firms use AI less often, but their use is growing.

## What the sources say

- In the Netherlands in 2025, 14% of businesses with 2–10 working persons used AI, against 27% at 10–50, 45% at 50–250 and 66% at 250+ [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Every size class roughly doubled its share between 2023 and 2025; the 2–10 group went from 7% to 14% and the 10–50 group from 11% to 27% [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- The strongest rise was among firms of 50–250 working persons, from 20% to 45% [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Specialised business services, our sector, rose from 13% in 2023 to 31% in 2025 [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
