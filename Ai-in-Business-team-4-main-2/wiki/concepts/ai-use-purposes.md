# AI use purposes

What firms actually use AI for: the business function where the AI is put to work, as opposed to the technology behind it.

## What the sources say

- Among Dutch businesses that used AI in 2025, the most common purposes were marketing or sales (35%) and business administration or management tasks (32%), followed by R&D or innovation (25%) [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Accounting, control or financial management came to 17%, ICT security 11% and logistics 4% [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Administration and management tasks include risk assessment, selecting applicants, translation and planning [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Over half of financial-services firms use AI for administration or management tasks [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
