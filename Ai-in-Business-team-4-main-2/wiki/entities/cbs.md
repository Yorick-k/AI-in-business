# CBS — Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek

Statistics Netherlands, the Dutch national statistics office. It publishes yearly figures on ICT and AI use by Dutch businesses, by firm size and sector.

## What the sources say

- CBS measures AI use among businesses with two or more working persons, across seven AI technologies, and reports figures for 2023–2025 [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Its 2025 figures are published as preliminary [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].

## Why it matters for us

An official, Dutch, recent source on exactly our target group (small business-service firms), which our research proposal names as a priority source.
