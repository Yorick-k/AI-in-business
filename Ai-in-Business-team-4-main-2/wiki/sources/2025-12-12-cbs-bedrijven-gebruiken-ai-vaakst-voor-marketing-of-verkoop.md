# Bedrijven gebruiken AI vaakst voor marketing of verkoop

A CBS news release on AI use by Dutch businesses with two or more working persons, 2023–2025. Dutch source; claims below are our English translation. 2025 figures are preliminary.

## TL;DR

- One in six Dutch businesses (17%) used AI in 2025, double the share of 2023 (8%).
- Use rises with firm size: 14% at 2–10 working persons, 27% at 10–50, 45% at 50–250, 66% at 250+.
- Firms that use AI do so most for marketing or sales (35%) and for administration or management tasks (32%).
- Text mining is the most used AI technology and grew fastest: from 4% of businesses in 2023 to 12% in 2025.
- Of firms that considered AI but do not use it, 73% name lack of experience as the main reason.

## Key claims

- Share of businesses (2+ working persons) using AI: 8% (2023), 13% (2024), 17% (2025*). — intro and table *Gebruik AI, bedrijfsgrootte*
- By size, 2023 → 2025*: 2–10 persons 7% → 14%; 10–50 persons 11% → 27%; 50–250 persons 20% → 45%; 250+ persons 42% → 66%. The rise is strongest at 50–250 persons. — table *Gebruik AI, bedrijfsgrootte*
- Purposes, as a share of businesses that use AI (2025*): marketing or sales 35%, business administration or management tasks 32% (such as risk assessment, selecting applicants, translation or planning), R&D or innovation 25%, production or service processes 18%, accounting, control or financial management 17%, ICT security 11%, logistics 4%. — section *Ruim 1 op 3 gebruikt AI voor marketing of verkoop*
- Over half of financial-services firms use AI for administration or management tasks; about 40% of firms in information and communication, and in rental and other business services, use it for marketing or sales. — same section
- AI technologies, share of all businesses, 2023 → 2025*: text mining 4% → 12%; natural language generation 3% → 8%; speech recognition 2% → 6% (tripled); machine learning 2% → 4%; robotic process automation 2% → 3%. — section *Vooral meer textmining en spraakherkenning*
- By sector (2025*): information and communication 54% (28% in 2023); specialised business services 31% (13% in 2023); financial services 28%; hospitality, transport and storage, and construction lowest at 6–7%. — section *Ruim helft bedrijven informatie en communicatie gebruikt AI*
- Three quarters of businesses do not use AI and have not considered it. Of those that considered it but do not use it: lack of experience 73%, privacy 49%, legal consequences such as liability for damage 42%; 15% say AI is not useful to them. — section *Gebrek aan ervaring belangrijkste reden geen AI-gebruik*

- 2025 figures are preliminary (*voorlopige cijfers*).
- Purposes are shares of AI-using businesses, not of all businesses.
- The population is businesses with two or more working persons; one-person firms are not included.
